SDR-Bench: Benchmarking the Personalization Capabilities of Large Language Models

Abstract: Personalization, the act of varying a message to induce action from a specific receiver while keeping sender, channel, and time fixed. We adapt the Bayesian Persuasion framework of Kamenica and Gentzkow (2011) to generative agents and instantiate the formulation in sales. We release SDR-Bench, a public corpus of 6,279 customer success stories spanning 22 industries and approximately 200 enterprises. Across frontier LLMs and deep-research agents, we observe a consistent personalization plateau.

Topics: personalization, LLM, Bayesian persuasion, benchmark, sales, user expectations
