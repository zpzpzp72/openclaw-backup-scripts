OPTScientist: Multi-Agent Discovery of Typed Optimizer Programs for Transformer Pretraining

Abstract: Designing optimizers for modern deep learning remains challenging. We introduce OPTScientist, a theory-guided multi-agent framework for optimizer discovery in a typed domain-specific language (DSL). Four role agents (Theorist, Designer, Engineer, Reviewer) collaborate within a single orchestration loop. Combines evolutionary search with DSL extension mechanism. Discovers RS-MR, a reduced-state matrix optimizer improving transformer pretraining.

Topics: multi-agent, optimizer discovery, typed DSL, evolutionary search, transformer pretraining
